About Me

I'm a Computer Science student with a passion for storytelling, programming, graphic design, gaming, and photography. I enjoy creating things—whether it's a fantasy novel, a software project, a cinematic game screenshot, or a digital artwork.

My Story

I've always enjoyed creating things. My journey started with a ghazal, then I switched to writing fantasy stories before expanding into graphic design, programming, and web development. Apart from that I research and experiment with multiple techniques regarding Lucid Dreams, creating my own theories and methods. Every new skill has become another way to bring ideas to life.

Writing

My first novel, The Huntsman, is a dark fantasy story that includes mystery, morality and the consequences of choice. It follows Thalion, known to the locals as The Huntsman, a lone ranger that wanders the wilds and shadowed roads, taking on contracts while searching for any trace of a man named Lucius Black... a name not uttered or heard by anyone for fourteen years. When The Huntsman stumbles upon a group of recently slain victims in the dark alleys of Umbra. On one of them, he notices a ring. A symbol that unlocks a buried memory, and with it, a long-lost clue about the man he has hunted for years. Little does he know that he is about to step onto a dark and dangerous path that may lead to a carnage the world is neither ready for nor capable of surviving.

Computer Science
