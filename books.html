The Huntsman

A dark fantasy novel about mystery, morality, forgotten memories, and the consequences of choice.

Synopsis

Thalion, known to the locals as The Huntsman, a lone ranger that wanders the wilds and shadowed roads, taking on contracts while searching for any trace of a man named Lucius Black... a name not uttered or heard by anyone for fourteen years. "The Huntsman" follows Thalion as he stumbles upon a group of recently slain victims in the dark alleys of Umbra. On one of them, he notices a ring. A symbol that unlocks a buried memory, and with it, a long-lost clue about the man he has hunted for years. Little does he know that he is about to step onto a dark and dangerous path that may lead to a carnage the world is neither ready for nor capable of surviving.

More About the Book

The Huntsman is the opening novel of a larger fantasy universe. It combines mystery, dark fantasy, moral conflict, and long-term worldbuilding into a story where every decision carries lasting consequences.
